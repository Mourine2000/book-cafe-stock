Book cafe stock & club member restock notification endpoints:

  • GET /health — Service health check
  • GET /version — Current application version
  • GET /books — List books and stock status
  • POST /books — Add a new book (JSON: {"title": "Piranesi", "author": "Susanna Clarke", "genre": "fantasy", "in_stock": true})
  • PATCH /books/<id>/stock — Update stock status (JSON: {"in_stock": true})
  • DELETE /books/<id> — Remove a book by ID
  • GET /members — List club members
  • POST /members — Subscribe a member (JSON: {"name": "Maya", "contact": "maya@example.com", "interest": "fantasy"})
  • GET /notifications — List triggered restock and new-arrival alerts
